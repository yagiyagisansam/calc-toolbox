Free calculators for everyday life in Japan. Every tool shows its formula and primary sources.

Health

  • Pure Alcohol Intake Calculator — Calculate the pure alcohol (g) and standard drinks in what you drink from the volume and ABV, free.
  • BMI & Ideal Weight Calculator — Enter your height and weight to get your BMI and ideal weight (BMI 22) for free, judged by the Japanese Society for the Study of Obesity standard used in Japanese health checkups.
  • Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) Calculator — Enter your age, sex and weight to calculate your daily basal metabolic rate (kcal) for free, using the reference values from Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Dietary Reference Intakes.
  • Caffeine Intake Calculator — Add up the caffeine in coffee, black tea, green tea, energy drinks and more, and compare it with the daily guide (400 mg for healthy adults, less during pregnancy).
  • Steps → Distance & Calorie Converter — Convert your step count and height into walking distance (km), time and estimated calories burned.
  • Due Date & Pregnancy Week Calculator — Calculate your due date and current pregnancy week (X weeks Y days) from the first day of your last period, free.
  • Daily Protein Intake Calculator — Calculate a daily protein guide from your weight and activity level, with food equivalents — how much chicken breast, eggs, milk or natto it corresponds to.
  • Target Heart Rate Calculator (Karvonen Formula) — Calculate the target heart-rate zone for fat burning and endurance with the Karvonen formula, from your age and resting heart rate.
  • Salt Content Calculator (Sodium ⇔ Salt Equivalent) — Convert the sodium (mg) on a food label into salt equivalent (g) in one step, free.
  • Sleep Duration Calculator — Just enter your bedtime and wake-up time to calculate your sleep duration for free — it works even across midnight.
  • Exercise Calorie Burn Calculator — Calculate the calories burned by 13 activities — jogging, cycling, swimming, tennis, strength training, jump rope, yoga and more — with the METs method (official METs table).
  • Daily Calorie Intake Calculator — Calculate for free how many calories you should eat per day (estimated energy requirement) from your age, sex, weight and activity level, based on the reference values and physical activity levels of Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare.
  • Walking Calorie Burn Calculator — Calculate the calories you burn walking from your weight, time and pace, using the METs formula from Japan's Ministry of Health guidelines.

  • Discount Calculator (How much is X% off?) — Find out instantly how much you pay after a 30% discount, and how much you save.

Dates & time

  • Age Calculator (Age, Chinese Zodiac, Hayaumare) — Enter your birth date to get your age today or on any reference date, your Chinese zodiac animal, and whether you are “hayaumare” (early-born) under Japan’s school-year system.
  • Days Between Dates Calculator — Count the days between two dates and get the date and weekday N days after (or before) a base date, free.
  • Japan Business Days Calculator — Find out when “3 business days later” falls, skipping weekends and Japanese national holidays (substitute holidays included).
  • Time Calculator (Totals & Time Difference) — Add up times like 1:30 + 0:45 + 8:00 and get the elapsed time between a start and end time.
  • Anniversary Day Counter — Enter a birthday or anniversary to see what day number today is and the dates of milestones such as day 100, day 1,000 and day 10,000 — free.
  • Add Months & Month Difference Calculator — Find the date N months after (or before) any date and the full-month difference between two dates, with automatic end-of-month adjustment — free.
  • Japanese Era (Wareki) Converter — Convert Western years to Japanese era years (Meiji, Taisho, Showa, Heisei, Reiwa) and back, instantly and free.

Everyday & conversion

  • Mean & Median Calculator — Paste a list of numbers to get the mean, median, max, min and sum in one go, for free.
  • Hensachi (Deviation Score) Calculator — Free calculator for hensachi, Japan's standardized exam score (mean 50, standard deviation 10).
  • Rice Gō ⇔ Gram Converter — Using the guides 1 gō of rice ≈ 150 g and about 200 ml of water per gō, get the rice weight, water amount, cooked yield and number of bowls from the gō count in one go.
  • Percentage Calculator — Free percentage calculator: what percent A is of B, X% of a value, and the percent increase or decrease from A to B — all in one place.
  • Dog & Cat Age Calculator (Human Years) — Convert the age of a cat or small-to-medium dog into human years for free: 1 year = about 15 human years, 2 years = about 24, then about +4 per year.
  • Star Sign & Birthstone Finder — Enter your birthday to instantly see your zodiac (star) sign and birthstone, free.
  • Height in Feet & Inches Converter — Convert height between centimeters and feet-and-inches notation (like 5'7″) in both directions.
  • Japanese Tablespoon, Teaspoon & Cup Converter — Convert between Japanese kitchen measures — 1 tablespoon = 15 ml, 1 teaspoon = 5 ml, 1 cup = 200 ml.
