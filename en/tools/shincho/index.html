What this tool tells you

See instantly what a height in centimeters becomes in the “5 feet 7 inches” style used in the US and other countries. It also converts the other way, from feet and inches to cm. Handy when reading overseas social-media profiles or when someone asks your height in English.

How to use

  1. Choose the direction of the conversion.
  2. Enter a height in cm, or in feet and inches.
  3. The result appears automatically the moment you type.

How it is calculated

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm (exact definitions)
  • Example: 170 cm ÷ 2.54 = 66.93 inches = 5 feet + 6.93 inches → rounded for notation to 5'7″
  • The “'” symbol means feet and “″” means inches.

Sources (primary information)

  • Pure arithmetic based on the exact definition of the international inch (1 inch = 2.54 cm).

Assumptions and notes

  • Feet-and-inches notation rounds the inches, so it is a “way of saying it” with about ±1 cm of leeway (5'7″ covers roughly 168.9–171.5 cm).

Frequently asked questions

How do I say 170 cm in the US?

Say “five seven” (5 feet 7). Precisely it is 66.93 inches, which rounds to 5'7″.

How many cm is 6 feet?

182.88 cm. In English-speaking countries “six feet” is a common benchmark for being tall.

Can it also convert weight to pounds?

This page converts height only. For reference, 1 pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg, so 1 kg is about 2.2 pounds.
