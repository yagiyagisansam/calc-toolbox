How to use it

  1. Tap "Create a poll" and enter your question and 2 to 10 options.
  2. Share the voting link you get on X (formerly Twitter), LINE, Instagram and so on.
  3. Anyone who opens the link can vote on the spot, and the results are tallied in real time as percentages and vote counts.

How the tally works

  • One vote per person (per device), one option each. Percentage (%) = that option's votes divided by all votes, times 100 (to one decimal place).
  • Respondents are the people the link was shared with. If you list the poll publicly, people who arrive from this page join in too (private polls are not listed). Either way, the figures are a rough guide, not a randomly sampled statistical survey.
  • Questions, options and vote counts are stored in an external database so the results can be tallied.

Please note

  • Do not put personal information (names, addresses, phone numbers and the like) in your question or options.
  • Polls that are abusive, discriminatory or otherwise inappropriate are deleted without notice, based on reports or on our own judgment.
  • Voting twice from the same device is blocked, but voting from a different device cannot be prevented. This tool is not suited to rigorous research.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything, or do I need an account?

It is free and needs no sign-up. Whether you are creating a poll or answering one, you just open a link.

How many options can I add?

Up to 10.

What if I do not want my poll listed publicly?

Turn off "List this poll publicly" when you create it, and it becomes a private poll that only people who know the link can open.

How do I delete a poll I made?

Open this page on the device you created it from and it appears under "Polls created on this device". Use "Delete" there to remove it yourself at any time (the votes it collected go with it). Only the creator and the site operator can delete a poll.

The questions, options and voting data that are created here are stored in an external database so the results can be tallied (no personally identifying information is collected). See our Privacy Policy for details.
